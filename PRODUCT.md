# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary**: Local sports fans and basketball enthusiasts in Nola and the Campania region checking fixtures, team rosters, match results, and club news.
- **Secondary**: Parents of youth players exploring the minibasket and youth academy programmes.
- **Tertiary**: Potential sponsors, institutional partners, and local businesses evaluating the club's brand reach and community impact.

## Product Purpose

The official digital home and front door for Hyria Basket (founded in 2025 in Nola). It connects the local community with the team, celebrates local sporting culture, drives match attendance, promotes youth basketball registration, and showcases the club's vision and sponsors with professional polish.

## Positioning

Rooted in the historical heritage of Hyria (the ancient city near Nola) while presenting a young, bold, forward-looking basketball project. Unlike generic sports club templates, it projects elite energy, professional craftsmanship, and deep community pride.

## Operating Context

- Primarily mobile-first casual browsing by fans and parents on the go.
- Fast access to upcoming games, team rosters, registration contacts, and club milestones.
- Event-driven traffic spikes around match days and youth registration campaigns.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Core Capabilities**: Team rosters, game calendar/results, club history/mission, youth academy details, contact & registration inquiries (EmailJS integration).
- **Constraints**: Dark-mode only aesthetic; fully responsive design prioritizing mobile ergonomics; strict adherence to performance and accessibility standards.
- **Terminology**: Italian language default (*"Giovane · Audace · Radicato"*, *"Minibasket"*, *"Settore Giovanile"*, *"Roster"*, *"Calendario/Risultati"*).

## Brand Commitments

- **Personality**: *Giovane · Audace · Radicato* (Young, Bold, Rooted). Direct, passionate, trustworthy, proud club tone in Italian.
- **Colors**: Deep maroon (`#5e0303`), vibrant accent orange (`#d96c00`), near-black background contrast (`#020617`), crisp light text (`#ffffff` / `#f8fafc`).
- **Typography**: Montserrat (body, UI, uppercase navigation) and Arvo (serif headings for emphasis).

## Product Principles

1. **Root Before Polish** — Every feature and layout reflects Nola's identity and community warmth rather than generic athletic tropes.
2. **Hierarchy Over Decoration** — Motion and color accents (orange) are purposeful cues guiding fans to key actions (rosters, fixtures, sign-ups), never gratuitous noise.
3. **Dark + Bold, Never Muddy** — Deep maroon canvas paired with sharp contrast ensures rich mood without compromising legibility.
4. **Trust Through Craft** — Consistency in spacing, typography, and responsive polish signals professional club management.
5. **Mobile-First Energy** — Smooth, touch-friendly interactions designed first for mobile fans.
